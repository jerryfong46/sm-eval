Tax note (Canada): for non-registered Smith Manoeuvre setups, many people prefer holdings with little or no return of capital (ROC) and more eligible Canadian dividends to reduce tax complexity.

Common Canadian starting points: XIU, XIC, VCN, ZCN (core Canadian equity), XEI, VDY, CDZ (dividend tilt), and VEQT/XEQT (one-ticket equity, but mixed distribution types).

Always verify each fund's latest annual tax breakdown from the ETF provider before buying.
